FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Short answers to the things people ask most. Type to filter.

Does pumFORGE work offline?
Yes. The engine, voice and image generation run entirely on your machine. There is no cloud dependency — nothing leaves your PC unless you explicitly enable an optional bridge or external API.
What are the hardware requirements?
Chat: 8 GB RAM (16 GB recommended), any x86_64 CPU, ~20 GB disk. Voice + images: NVIDIA 8 GB VRAM confirmed working. Full pipeline: NVIDIA 12 GB+ VRAM and 16 GB RAM. See Download for the full table.
How do I install it?
Linux: curl -fsSL https://pumforge.com/install.sh | sh or install the .deb. Windows: run the installer (beta). macOS: coming soon. Full instructions are on the Download page.
Is my data really private?
Yes. Telemetry is off by default and never required. Your chats, files and voice stay on your hardware. The only PII the product stores is what you choose to sign up for (e.g. the launch waitlist), which is GDPR-compliant double opt-in.
Does it cost anything?
The local engine is free — the only cost is electricity. Plus licenses are a flat annual fee, no per-seat metering. If you add an optional cloud model you bring your own key and can estimate the cost on the Predictable Cost page.
Can I use my own model?
Yes. pumFORGE runs llama.cpp; download any GGUF from the Model Library or paste a HuggingFace URL. It ships with a local default so you're ready in minutes.
How does voice cloning work?
Record or drop a 15–20 second sample and pumFORGE clones the voice locally with one of six engines. The voice is then used by Chat, the Voice page and Forgey. Nothing is uploaded.
What is Forgey?
Forgey is your on-device dev co-worker sharing your live terminal. He sees your cwd and recent output, proposes commands with Run / Dry-Run / Copy, and only executes after your consent. Everything is audited.
Can pumFORGE schedule tasks?
Yes — Settings → Automation. Consent-gated, dry-run-first scheduled jobs (nightly lint, builds, patches). Failures are surfaced, never silent.
Does it work with Slack or Discord?
Optionally. Enable the bridge in Settings → Integrations, paste a webhook URL and set a token. Same audited flows, controlled from chat. Opt-in, so messages transit the provider only when you choose.
How do I get a bug fixed?
Open an issue on GitHub or email bugreports@pumforge.com with a reproducible step-by-step. Include the engine log: sudo journalctl -u pumforge.
Is there a mobile version?
Not yet — the web app works great in a mobile browser over your LAN. A mobile app is on the roadmap.
How do I uninstall or wipe everything?
Stop the service (sudo systemctl stop pumforge), remove the package, and delete the data folder (~/talk or /opt/pumforge depending on install). Settings → Data & Privacy has per-category wipes too.
What does the "pf" logo mark mean?
The pf mark is pumFORGE's product monogram for small UI contexts (settings, tray, favicon). The robot is the full brand mark for the site and marketing. See our docs.
