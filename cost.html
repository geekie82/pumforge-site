Predictable cost

Know the price before you install.

pumFORGE runs 100% on your machine — no tokens, no metering, no surprise bills. Inference is local, telemetry is off by default, and nothing leaves your PC unless you explicitly enable an optional bridge or API.

Zero telemetry

Anonymous telemetry is off by default and never required. There is no tracking, no analytics firehose, no "phone home".

No data leakage

Your chats, files and voice stay on your hardware. Optional integrations (email, bridges, search) are opt-in, audited, and clearly labelled.

Local inference

The engine runs on your CPU/GPU. Electricity is your only cost. No per-token billing, no API keys required.

Optional APIs

The honest version

With the local engine you pay zero per token. The only recurring cost is electricity. If you add an optional external model, you control the key, the model, and can delete it any time — no lock-in, no metered surprise, and every call is audited in your local log.

For team licenses, see Pricing — a single flat annual fee. There is no per-seat metering and no usage-based billing.
