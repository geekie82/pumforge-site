FAQ

Does it need the internet?

No. The model, speech, and everything else run locally on your machine. You only need internet if you use optional tools like web search, weather, or stock prices.

Is my data private?

Yes. Chats, files, memories, and your cloned voice stay on your own disk. Nothing is sent to a pumTALK cloud (there is none). If you configure a cloud LLM provider (OpenAI, Anthropic), messages are sent to that provider for inference only.

What hardware do I need?

An 8GB+ machine is fine for text. For the best voice experience, an NVIDIA GPU (12GB+) is recommended for faster speech and cloning.

Which AI model does it use?

It works with Ollama or LM Studio, so you can use any local model you like and swap models freely. Cloud providers (OpenAI, Anthropic) are also supported.

Can it really control my PC?

Yes — files, apps, windows, clipboard, screenshots, OCR, volume/brightness, and more. It runs freely with natural confirmations, but security-critical changes (editing sshd_config, enabling root login, sudoers) ask for your approval first. Desktop control is opt-in.

How does it know my location for weather?

On first load pumTALK asks for your browser's geolocation (a normal "allow location" prompt) and uses your real location for weather and forecasts. The desktop app can share the PC's location too.

Does the wake word work with a silent mic?

Yes. With Wake word enabled, say "Hey Pum" to wake pumTALK even in push-to-talk mode with the mic off.

How does the license work?

You get a 7-day free trial with all features. After the trial, a 3-day grace period follows. Then Voice, Notebook, and Tools are disabled. Basic chat always works. Enter a license key in Settings to restore full features.

What voice cloning engines are supported?

Six engines: F5-TTS (best quality), XTTS v2 (fastest), IndexTTS-2, Chatterbox, FishSpeech, and CosyVoice. Clone in ~15 seconds from a recording or uploaded file.

Can I use it on a server?

Yes. pumTALK runs as a systemd service on Linux. Bind to 0.0.0.0 for LAN access with authentication. Docker images are also available.
