The headline feature

Meet Forgey — your AI co-worker.

Not a chatbot. A teammate who shares your live shell, watches the repo, and helps you build — without ever leaving your machine.

One shell. Two collaborators.

Forgey sees your current directory, your recent terminal output and your repo index — so it answers about the real state of your session, not a guess.

Proposes. You run.

Ask in plain English and Forgey drafts real shell commands with a Run / Dry-Run / Copy button and a risk tag. Nothing executes without you.

Consent-first safety

Risky actions always ask first — Run once, Trust always, or Cancel — with per-command always-allow and a Suggestion-only lock.

Explain, debug, plan

“Explain the last error”, “what should I run next?”, “summarise this repo” — with syntax-highlighted command cards you can execute, preview read-only, or copy.

Settings Hub

Twelve categories with per-section saves and conflict protection, plus a live Hardware monitor for CPU, RAM, VRAM and detected GPUs.

Engine under your control

GPU backend selection (auto / CUDA / Vulkan / CPU), idle auto-unload, model-load guardrails with preflight estimates, and server-side Test-connection.
