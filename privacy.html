Privacy

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is simple: pumFORGE runs entirely on your machine.

What we store

pumFORGE keeps your chats, memories and settings on your own disk. Nothing is uploaded to any cloud service.

No telemetry

By default pumFORGE sends no analytics, no crash reports, and no usage data. If you enable anonymous telemetry, it is optional and can be turned off anytime in Settings.

Voice & camera

Voice and camera processing happens locally. When you use the browser mic or camera, it is on your device only.

Your data, your rules

You can export or delete your local data at any time. Factory reset wipes everything back to first run.
